## Part A: Terms of Use

### New section: Making Tax Digital (insert after section 4, renumber the rest)

**Making Tax Digital**

If your plan includes it, Evolution can send your quarterly updates and your annual tax return to HMRC under Making Tax Digital for Income Tax.

- **Linking your HMRC account.** You link your account by signing in on HMRC's own website. Evolution never sees your HMRC password. You can unlink at any time in the app; this stops Evolution sending anything further, but doesn't change what HMRC already holds.
- **What you authorise.** By linking your account, you authorise Evolution to send the quarterly updates and tax return you choose to send, and to retrieve HMRC's tax calculations and your update deadlines on your behalf. Evolution acts as your software, not as your agent or accountant.
- **Your figures.** Every update is worked out from the records you have entered in Evolution. Before anything is sent, you are shown the figures. **You are responsible for checking them and for what is sent to HMRC**, as you would be for any tax return.
- **Tax return eligibility.** Evolution can only file a complete tax return if your only income for the year is the self-employment you record in it. You must answer the eligibility questions truthfully. If they don't apply to you, you must file your return another way.
- **The declaration.** Submitting your tax return includes a declaration, made by you to HMRC, that the information is correct and complete. Evolution records that you agreed to it and when.
- **Deadlines.** You remain responsible for meeting HMRC's deadlines. Evolution shows the dates HMRC gives us, but we can't guarantee you'll see them, or that HMRC's systems will be available when you try to send.
- **Corrections.** Updates are cumulative: correcting your records and sending your next update replaces earlier figures at HMRC. After your tax return is submitted, corrections are made by amending the return.
- **Records.** HMRC requires you to keep your digital records for at least 5 years after the 31 January deadline for the tax year. Evolution keeps the years you have sent to HMRC protected from "Reset all data". **If you delete your Evolution account, your records are deleted too, so export them first.**
- **HMRC's calculations are HMRC's.** Tax calculations shown from HMRC are produced by HMRC from the information it holds.

## Part B: Privacy Policy

### Section 2: new subsection G, Making Tax Digital (only if you link your HMRC account)

- Your **National Insurance number**, which HMRC uses to identify you. Stored encrypted.
- The **access keys HMRC issues** when you link your account. Stored encrypted; never your HMRC password.
- Your **business identifier at HMRC**, and the **deadlines** HMRC sets for your updates.
- A **copy of every update and tax return sent**, with the date, HMRC's reference and, for a tax return, the declaration you agreed to and your answers to the eligibility questions.
- **Fraud-prevention information.** HMRC requires all MTD software, by law, to send details about the device and connection used with each request: a random identifier for your phone (created by Evolution), your phone's operating system, model, screen and window size, time zone, local and public IP address, the app's version, your Evolution account identifiers, and, if you signed in with two-factor authentication, when and how. **[Solicitor]** Confirm the legal basis wording (HMRC fraud-prevention header requirements for MTD).

### Section 3: add to "How we use your information"

- Send your quarterly updates and tax return to HMRC when you choose to, and show you HMRC's calculations and deadlines.
- Send HMRC the fraud-prevention information it requires with each request.

### Section 5: add to "Sharing and disclosures"

"If you link your HMRC account, we send HMRC the updates and tax return you choose to submit, your National Insurance number, and the fraud-prevention information described in section 2G. HMRC uses this information as a separate controller under its own privacy notice."

### Section 6: add to "Data retention"

"Copies of updates and tax returns sent to HMRC are kept while your account exists, and years you have sent to HMRC are protected from 'Reset all data'. They are deleted with your account; HMRC keeps its own copy."
