Request Account Deletion

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Submit this form to request permanent deletion of your Evolution account and all associated data — expenses, receipts, income records, and tax summaries. This works even if you no longer have the app installed.

Required to confirm this request actually comes from you, not just anyone who knows your email.
After submitting this form, your request is queued for processing within 30 days. If your password doesn't match our records, the request will be rejected — contact support@aplccommodities.com if you've lost access to your account.
